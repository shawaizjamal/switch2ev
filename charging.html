Steps To Install a Charger

Installing an EV charger at your home or business in London involves a few key steps:
1. Assess Your Needs: Determine the type of charger you need (Level 2 for faster charging) and your electrical capacity.
2. Find an Installer: Use a certified electrician familiar with EV charger installations and local London regulations.
3. Check Planning Permission: In some cases, you may need planning permission from your local London council. Check with them early in the process.
4. Installation: The installer will handle the electrical work, mounting the charger, and ensuring it's safely connected.
5. Testing and Clarification: Your installer will test the charger and provide you with the necessary certification.
6. Available Grants: Check if you're eligible for any government grants or funding available in London to help with the cost of installation.


Types Of Chargers

Home Charger

Usually charge your EV at home overnight. Level 1 (120V) or Level 2 (240V) options available. Ideal for daily commuting and topping up your battery.

Public/Residential Area Charger

Charge your EV while you shop, work, or live. Found in parking lots, shopping centers, apartments, and workplaces. Supports Level 2 charging for a faster charge than at home.

Fast Charger

Get back on the road quickly with DC fast chargers. Located along highways and at dedicated charging stations. Provides a significant charge in a short amount of time for long-distance travel.
