EV and PHEV Information

How EVs & PHEV Work

Batteries are the only source of electricity used by electric vehicles (EVs). Instead of using a normal engine, these batteries power an electric motor. Plug-in hybrid electric cars, or PHEVs, contains characteristics of both, gasoline vehicles and electric vehicles. They have a gasoline engine for longer journeys and an electric motor that runs on batteries for shorter ones. As EVs, PHEVs can be charged by plugging in, and they are also able to switch between both, gasoline and electricity, when needed. For daily transportation, this dual-system method provides the advantages of electric driving, while for longer trips, it delivers the range of a gasoline-powered vehicle.

Benefits Of EV

Low Maintenance Cost

As there are less moving parts, electric vehicles (EVs) do not require complicated transmission repairs, spark plug replacements, or oil changes. For EV owners, this means significantly less long-term maintenance expenses.

Tax Incentives & Savings

Governments reduce the initial cost of EVs by providing a range of financial incentives, such as tax credits and refunds. If we compare EVs with reduced fuel and maintenance costs, they provide significant long-term savings.

Eco Friendly

As EVs produce no harmful gases, the air is cleaner and the environment is healthier. EVs contribute heavily to climate change prevention and green transportation by decreasing the need for fossil fuels.
